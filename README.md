# K-Food Growth Intelligence & Product Opportunity Simulator

An ML-assisted business analytics project that combines real public macroeconomic and consumer search trend data with synthetic anonymized marketplace and review data to identify K-Food product opportunities across selected markets.

## Project Objective

This project simulates how a business planning or market intelligence team could evaluate K-Food product opportunities using:

- Google Trends consumer interest signals
- World Bank macroeconomic indicators
- Synthetic anonymized competitor benchmark data
- Synthetic product review corpus
- ML-assisted review topic modeling
- Country-market clustering
- Product opportunity scoring
- Scenario-based business planning simulation

## Data Transparency

This project uses a hybrid data strategy:

### Real Public Data
- World Bank macroeconomic indicators
- Google Trends search interest data

### Synthetic Data
- Anonymized competitor product benchmark
- Synthetic product reviews
- Scenario-based business assumptions

Synthetic data are used to simulate business planning workflows when proprietary marketplace, company sales, and customer-level data are unavailable.
